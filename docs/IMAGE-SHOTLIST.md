# Image shot list

The site currently uses graded Unsplash stock (see `image-credits.md`). Stock
is a stand-in. **Advisor Technologies' own photographs (real projects, real
installations, the real team and office) will always look better and be more
credible than any stock image.** Photograph the slots below and drop the
files in with the same names; nothing else needs to change.

How to shoot:
- Daylight or bright, even light. Avoid harsh flash and coloured light.
- Tidy the frame: cable-managed racks, clean floors, no clutter.
- Landscape, at least 2400px on the long side, sharp.
- People are welcome. Get consent, and show safety gear on industrial sites.
- After replacing a file, run it through the same grade: put it in
  `scripts/process-photos.mjs` or match its look (bright, cool, slightly
  desaturated).
